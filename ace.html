What Ace is

The Sim Ledger logs every edge the NBA and NFL sims find, often 35–55 a night. Ace is the short list: it reads the same rows right after each pricing run and keeps only the picks that still have an edge once the sim is pulled toward the market. One paper bankroll covers both leagues. Ace never places a bet; this is a public, timestamped test.

How a pick qualifies

  • Pool: every player prop and game total the sim priced (spreads and moneylines are out).
  • Trust the sim only part way. Where the sim and the market disagree, the market has mostly been right. So Ace uses market + k × (sim − market), with k fit each night on every pick already graded. A k of 0.20 means Ace believes the sim 20% of the way.
  • Edge: that number must beat the market’s no-vig chance by 7 points on props, 6 on totals, and be +EV at the listed price.
  • Limits: one pick per player, one total per game, no nightly count. NFL picks flagged for a role or roster question are left out.
  • Late news: the pre-game re-check redoes each open pick with the latest injury news and the current market. If the player is ruled out, the line has moved a point or more against the pick, or the pick no longer clears the bar, Ace pulls it before the game. A pulled pick returns its stake and is not a win or a loss.

Stakes and the bankroll

The bankroll starts at 100 units (1u = $100 by default; type your own unit size at the top). Each pick stakes one-tenth Kelly on Ace’s probability, at most 3u, and a night’s stakes are capped at 30u (best EV first). Stakes are sized off the starting bankroll. Pushes and voids return the stake.

Live vs backfill

Live picks are made before the games, from Nov 4, 2026 on. Backfill picks (Oct 24 – Nov 3) were rebuilt from the ledger by the same rule, using only results known before each night. They show how the rule would have done; they were not posted at the time.

Reading the page

  • CLV: the closing no-vig chance at Ace’s line minus the chance its price implied. Above zero means Ace beat the closing market. Taking the closing price itself scores about −2.5 to −3.5, the vig.
  • Why on any pick shows the sim, the market, Ace’s blend, the edge and the stake.
