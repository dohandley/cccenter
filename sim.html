How to read this
  • Every game is simulated play by play thousands of times (possessions for the NBA, snaps for the NFL), using that day's injury report and projected rotations. The big numbers are the average simulated score.
  • Bets are 1 unit each. A bet needs the model to beat the no-vig market by 6 points on totals or 7 on props, and it still has to clear breakeven in the stress test, which reruns the game with every team and player edge cut in half. Leans are edges that didn't qualify, tracked to see whether they would have paid.
  • Each meter is a win probability, and the dark tick is breakeven at the posted price. In the box score, actuals are green when they beat the projection and red when they fell short.
  • Sides (spreads and moneylines) are tracked as leans only. In backtesting the model didn't beat the closing spread, so they aren't staked.
  • Prices are from when the game was priced. For entertainment; not betting advice.
