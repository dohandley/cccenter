How to read this
  • Every game is simulated possession by possession, 4,000 times, with that day's injury report and projected rotations. The big numbers are the average simulated score.
  • Bets are 1 unit each and need the model to beat the no-vig market by 6 points on totals or 7 on props, and still clear breakeven in the stress test, which reruns the game with every team and player edge cut in half. Leans are edges that didn't qualify, tracked for reference.
  • Each meter is a win probability; the dark tick is breakeven at the posted price. In the box score, actuals are green when they beat the projection and red when they fell short.
  • Prices are from when the game was priced. For entertainment; not betting advice.
